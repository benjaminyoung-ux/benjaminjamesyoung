01 The challenge

Customers were struggling to discover the right products.

The existing experience created friction at several points in the journey. Customers had access to a huge product catalogue, but finding products relevant to their needs wasn't always straightforward.

The opportunity was to simplify discovery while creating a more personalised experience.

02 The opportunity

The data highlighted a clear opportunity.

+23% potential conversion opportunity
2.4m annual sessions affected

04 Research & insight

We found that customers needed more confidence, not more choice.

Analysis of behavioural data, customer feedback and usability sessions revealed a consistent pattern.

05 The work

Turning insight into a simpler experience.

We redesigned the experience around clearer hierarchy, stronger recommendations and more useful product information.
